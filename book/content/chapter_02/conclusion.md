## Conclusion

We set out in this chapter to build a new version of a classic model from first principles. The apparatus developed in these pages is that which we intend to query, closely and extensively, over the subsequent chapters.

However, before we can produce our first model results, there is one more piece of equipment we sorely need. We have stated our intent to 'do things differently' by exploring a classic model in great depth instead of a newer model in lesser depth. If we are to do this successfully, we need an apparatus that can overpower the technical and practical obstacles that stand athwart such an approach - not just for the particular use-case of geophysics, but for all models of a similar 'shape'. Our answer to this need provides the subject of the next chapter.

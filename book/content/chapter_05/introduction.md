# Introduction

The behaviour of any fluid under thermal convection is determined by its rheology: the particular laws that govern the manner and rate at which momentum is diffused. The simplest rheologies are linearly dependent on the variables of state.

In this chapter, we will apply our *Everest*, *PlanetEngine*, and *Starling* systems to study two specific linear rheologies: the isoviscous rheology and an Arrhenius-type (exponentially temperature-dependent) rheology. For each, we will compare our results to the standard benchmarks for Cartesian models before going into the annulus, where we will attempt to analyse our uncommonly large and detailed dataset to extract more rigorous and precise scaling laws than have previously been known.
